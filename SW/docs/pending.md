| Lock the QR scanner against setting codes | Added on 2026-11-17, open. The GM861 obeys any of its setting codes it sees (zone bit 0x0003 bit 1 = 0 "Open", as read on an old and a new sensor): a hostile QR (on a screen, a page, a sticker) can change its speed, mode or tail and leave the scanner useless until it is set up again with the manual codes, or with a 9600 build (denial of service; no secret is reached). Close them (0x0003 bit 1 = 1). How is the user's choice: the firmware writes it to the scanner RAM at every power-on, before any scan (keeps the rule that it never writes the scanner flash; a sensor set up again by QR before that cannot happen, the device scans nothing before), or once in its flash at manufacturing (the "Close Setting Code" of the manual; then a new sensor can only be set up over the UART).
